\section*{Use of AI Tools}
The experiments, analyses, figures and research records in this work were
carried out by an AI coding agent operating on the authors' hardware under a
written protocol: every measurement task registers its decision criteria,
predictions and experimental grid in a commit before measurement begins, and
each task is recorded with its raw artefacts, invariant checks and the layer at
which each finding is claimed to hold. The research direction, the choice of
questions, the preregistration criteria and every judgement reported here are
the authors'. The agent did not select which results to report, and no
criterion was relaxed after measurement; where one was corrected, the original
criterion's failure is reported alongside it (Appendix~B).
