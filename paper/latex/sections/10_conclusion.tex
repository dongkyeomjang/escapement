\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:10}

Agentic serving costs are set by an arrival process, and on a substrate that freezes its batch widths and its slot pool at compile time, that arrival process acts through three mechanisms: alignment with a discrete grid, a cliff in a sequence-granular slot pool, and a prefill that is charged to every concurrent session. We established the first causally by recompiling only the grid, and built a step-level model of all three with no fitted parameters that passed preregistered out-of-sample, intervention and workload-transfer gates.
% CLAIMS 1.6, 1.14, 1.15, 1.16, 1.17

Used as an instrument rather than a demonstration, that model produced a negative result we did not want. Rescheduling returns leaves real headroom, but a median 60 \% of it is coordination that no per-session policy can reach even with omniscient knowledge; the value of return-time information evaporates under measured tool latencies; and the published estimator for the quantity one would need misses its threshold by a margin more data does not close.
% CLAIMS 2.5, 2.6, 2.7, 2.8

What survives is a lever that needs a distribution and not a draw. Choosing the compiled configuration from workload statistics alone --- no device measurement in the selection loop --- and applying it with an eight-minute recompile recovered 9.7--10.1 \% of device time at eight concurrent sessions on two independently registered channels. We call that pair --- the model and the search built on it --- \textbf{Escapement}. Pushing the same lever further showed it stopping at a pool size of sixteen, where the cache survival rate reaches one hundred percent, at one third of what the device could physically hold.
% CLAIMS 3.2, 3.4
% CLAIMS 3.11, 3.13

The result we would most like to be carried elsewhere is not the number. It is the shape: \textbf{the gain is proportional to the recoverable cache loss, and it ends when survival saturates} --- reproduced three times, twice where it predicted a gain and once where it predicted none. And the method that made the last of those checkable: a model that predicts the \emph{absence} of an effect, committed before measurement, is evidence of a kind that a model reproducing its own training conditions cannot supply.
